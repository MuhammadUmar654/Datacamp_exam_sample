The biggest revenue pool is not the highest-spending customer group.

Guests with no loyalty tier contribute the most revenue in total. Platinum guests show the highest average recorded spending per purchaser.

The comparison is observational, not evidence that loyalty upgrades cause spending.

09 / Director decision

Approve a measured pilot—not an untested rollout.

Proposed action

  • Test a targeted ancillary-service offer with eligible guests.
  • Consider a separate loyalty-enrolment hypothesis.
  • Use a comparable control group where feasible.

Decision metrics

  • Campaign purchase conversion rate.
  • Revenue per eligible guest, including zero spenders.
  • Incremental revenue versus control and campaign cost.
Decision requested: authorise a small pilot and agree on success thresholds before scaling.
